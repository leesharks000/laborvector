 Resource Library
 The following are a list of resources which the Luddite Lab team has reviewed and often used in conversations around generative AI in the workplace. The resources include research-based and policy-centered primers, organizing toolkits aimed at mobilizing non-unionized and precarious workers, tools for starting conversations with colleagues about generative AI, and repositories and examples of draft contract language for technology governance. We highlight those resources which we find to be helpful and actionable as organizing tools. This means we typically shy away from general statements of principles.
