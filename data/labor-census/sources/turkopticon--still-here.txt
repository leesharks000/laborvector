 Turkopticon is Still Here!

 Turkopticon

 Turkopticon

 Organizing data workers to hold platforms, companies, and institutions accountable to the labor they rely on.

 Published Sep 4, 2026

 + Follow

 For many workers, Amazon Mechanical Turk has been a familiar part of the platform landscape for years. With Amazon announcing that MTurk will close on September 30, 2026, we know many workers may be wondering what that means for Turkopticon. 

 The short answer is that we are still here . 

 Turkopticon may have started as a resource for MTurk workers, but our work has changed and expanded over the years. As data work moved onto more platforms, we did too. 

 Today, Turkopticon supports and advocates for platform data workers wherever they are working. That includes people doing AI training and evaluation, data annotation, surveys, transcription, content moderation, and other forms of platform-based data work. 

 We know how quickly this industry can change. Platforms open and close. Work disappears. Workers can be suspended or deactivated with little or no explanation. And too often they are left trying to navigate those dangers on their own. We don’t think workers should have to do that alone. 

 Turkopticon will continue to provide support, share information and resources, listen to workers’ concerns, and advocate for better treatment across the platform data work industry. We also continue working to build relationships with platforms so that workers’ concerns can be heard directly and we can push for fairer policies, clearer communication, and better treatment when problems arise. 

 If MTurk has been your primary platform and you're now figuring out your next steps, you’re welcome here. If you’ve already moved on to other platforms, you’re welcome here too. 

 Turkopticon isn’t tied to one platform. We’re here for the workers, and that isn’t changing. 

 –The Turkopticon Team
