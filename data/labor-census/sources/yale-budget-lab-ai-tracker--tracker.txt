 Tracking the Impact of AI on the Labor Market

 October 14, 2026

 | Updated: October 14, 2026

 Key Takeaways 

 The occupational mix is not yet changing in ways that clearly align with the introduction of AI into the workforce.

 Measures of AI usage show no connection to changes in employment or unemployment.

 A synthetic differences-in-differences analysis of AI exposure does not yet clearly indicate an AI-related labor market footprint.

 We plan on updating this analysis regularly moving forward to see how the impact of AI on the labor market changes over time.
