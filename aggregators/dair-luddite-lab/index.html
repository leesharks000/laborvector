Luddite Lab Resource Hub: a resource hub (case studies, primers and a resource library), one of the Luddite Lab's two offerings; the other is trainings for unions, labor organizations and worker organizations around automation and AI. Operator: Distributed AI Research Institute (DAIR); the hub's About page lists Alex Hanna (Director of Research), Adrienne Williams and Hannah Lipstein as its team, and AI Weekly reports the team as led by Alex Hanna and Hannah Lipstein. Its unit: a resource: a case study of worker organizers resisting AI, a primer, or a reviewed resource in the library (research and policy primers, organizing toolkits, political education, contract language, alternative technology projects). Whose labor: the work of union members and worker-organizers facing AI, automation and workplace surveillance, in industries the hub names as journalism, entertainment and nursing.
