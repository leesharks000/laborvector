Luddite Lab Resource Hub: a resource hub (case studies, primers and a resource library). Operator: Distributed AI Research Institute (DAIR). Its unit: a resource: a case study of worker organizers resisting AI, a primer, or a reviewed resource in the library (research and policy primers, organizing toolkits, political education, contract language, alternative technology projects). Whose labor: the work of union members and worker-organizers facing AI, automation and workplace surveillance, in industries the hub names as journalism, entertainment and nursing.

The Luddite Lab Resource Hub provides resources for unions, labor organizations, and organizers fighting AI and automation at work.

What this evidence can settle

Can answer

  • which organizing strategies workers have used to push back against AI and automation at work, as the case studies tell them
  • where to find organizing toolkits and examples of pro-worker contract language on automation and AI

Cannot answer

  • how widespread any strategy is, or its measured outcome
  • any quantity of exposure, adoption or employment change

In the worked questions

  • Where are actual negotiated protections against automated monitoring and discipline?
