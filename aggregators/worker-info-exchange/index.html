Worker Info Exchange

AI governing laborWorker powernon-profit organisationactive
Worker Info Exchange: a non-profit organisation: data access requests, data investigations, data trusts and strategic litigation for gig workers. Operator: Worker Info Exchange, a non-profit in London; the Uber collective action is brought by Stichting WIE International. Its unit: a subject access or data portability request made on behalf of a worker; also a targeted data investigation or report. Whose labor: the work of gig economy workers managed by algorithms (Uber drivers in the UK and Europe named in its news; its reports include a Just Eat Report and a McMisery Report).

Worker Info Exchange is a non-profit helping workers reclaim control over the data collected from them at work. We help workers turn data into power — challenging unfair algorithmic systems and holding tech platforms to account.

In its own words: https://www.workerinfoexchange.org/, read 2026-10-29

Who supplies, who directs, who gains

Supplies the laborgig economy workers, as the sources say, Uber drivers among them
Directs ittech platforms through opaque automated management systems (hidden algorithms) that allocate work, determine pay and assess performance
Receives the gains, bears the costsnot recorded as a quantity in the saved sources; it names algorithmic wage cuts and damages drivers have suffered from Uber's dynamic pay system, and news titles report falling pay and soaring commission fees

What this evidence can settle

Can answer

  • what data a platform holds and uses about an individual worker, as returned to a request
  • how a platform's algorithm allocated work, set pay and assessed performance for the workers whose data was obtained

Cannot answer

  • platform-wide pay or dismissal rates beyond the workers whose data is aggregated
  • the outcome of the Uber collective action (pending in the saved sources)

In the worked questions

  • What does automated management of workers look like, and can a worker see the data it uses?

Where it meets Labor Vector

rd none stated. The archive reads WIE's data access as recovering, from the worker's side, the record of how an algorithm directed the work (allocation, pay, performance), the directionality question asked of the system that directs labor.
